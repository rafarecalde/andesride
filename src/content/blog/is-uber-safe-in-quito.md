Safety is a fair question to ask before you land somewhere new, and Quito is no exception. Here's a practical, non-alarmist take on getting around — and on arrival in particular.

## The honest picture

Quito is a major capital that millions of travelers visit without incident, and most rides — by taxi or app — are perfectly fine. That said, like any large city, it pays to use normal street-smarts, and **the airport arrival is the moment to be most deliberate**, because you're tired, carrying valuables, and unfamiliar with the city.

## Reducing the risk on arrival

- **Don't accept rides from people approaching you inside the terminal.** Use only official, marked options or a pre-arranged driver.
- If you use an app, **confirm the car, plate, and driver name** match before getting in, and share your trip with someone.
- Keep valuables out of sight and your bags with you.
- Have your **hotel address written down**, ideally in Spanish.
- Be aware that **rideshare pickup at the airport is restricted** and the rules shift, so don't count on it as your only plan. ([More on Uber at UIO.](/guide/uber-from-quito-airport))

## Why a pre-booked transfer is the low-risk arrival

The single biggest safety upgrade for arrival is removing the curbside lottery entirely. With a private transfer:

- The driver is **vetted and airport-authorized**, not whoever happens to be there. ([Why that matters.](/guide/why-licensed-airport-drivers-matter-quito))
- They're **expecting you specifically**, holding a sign with your name. ([How meet & greet works.](/guide/dont-arrive-to-an-empty-airport))
- Everything — driver, car, price — is **known before you land**, so there's nothing to negotiate while jet-lagged.

## Bottom line

Quito is very visitable, and rideshare isn't inherently unsafe — but the arrival is when uncertainty is highest and good options are thinnest, especially [late at night](/guide/arriving-quito-airport-late-at-night). Pre-booking a licensed transfer is the simplest way to make your first hour in Ecuador a non-event.
