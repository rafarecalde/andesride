United Airlines flight **UA1002** is the evening nonstop from Houston George Bush (IAH) to **Mariscal Sucre (UIO)**. A published schedule effective 8 September 2026 through 24 October 2026 lists an 18:05 departure from Houston and a **23:25 arrival in Quito — 11:25pm local**. A public flight-status page for 4 October 2026 showed the same scheduled gate arrival, 23:25. From 25 October 2026 the same flight is listed a few minutes later, around 11:30pm, on most days of the week. Confirm the time on [united.com](https://www.united.com) before you fly. Schedules move, and a delay out of Houston can push you past midnight.

This is the landing the late-night transfer is built for. You have been in the air more than five hours. Immigration, bags, and a dark highway are still between you and a bed.

## Immigration and bags after an 11:25pm arrival

The international hall at this hour is quieter than the afternoon. Fewer flights are on the ground, and fewer staff are at the desks. A quiet hall is not the same thing as a fast hall. One officer and a full 737 can still mean a wait. Keep your passport out. If you are connecting ideas about dinner or a meeting the next morning, let that go until you have the bags.

The belt is the other variable. Even when the aircraft is on time, bags can sit. Do not walk outside to “see about a taxi” and leave someone with the luggage. Stay together until the last bag is on a cart.

Outside, the official taxi area is thinner than it is at 4pm. That is when unsolicited offers get louder, not quieter. [Hailing at the curb](/guide/how-much-is-a-taxi-from-quito-airport) means a conversation you do not want to have in a second language while you are tired. Walk only to a driver holding your name. The handoff is described in [meet and greet at UIO](/guide/dont-arrive-to-an-empty-airport).

Earlier the same evening, [American AA833 from Miami](/guide/american-airlines-aa833-miami-to-quito-night) typically lands closer to 9pm. If your ticket is the Houston flight, plan for 11:25pm, not for that earlier bank.

## The highway into Cumbayá or Quito

UIO is in Tababela. The highway west — Ruta Viva — drops you toward Cumbayá and then climbs into Quito. Around midnight the road is usually moving. A common nighttime run into the city is about **35–45 minutes**. Cumbayá, which sits on that valley path, is often closer to 25–35 minutes. A Quito address in mixed daytime traffic is published as about 40–60 minutes, so do not use a rush-hour memory of the airport road to judge this arrival. Wyndham Quito Airport, in Tababela, is typically 10–15 minutes if you are stopping there instead of going into town.

Altitude is real on the first night. If someone in your group feels off, the right move is a slow walk to a car that is already paid for, not a search for the cheapest ride.
