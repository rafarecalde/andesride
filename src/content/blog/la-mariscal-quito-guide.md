If the Old Town is Quito's history, La Mariscal is its night out. This central district — and the artsy **La Floresta** beside it — is where travelers base themselves for restaurants, bars, cafés, and easy access to the rest of the city.

## What's the vibe

**La Mariscal** has long been the traveler hub, centered on **Plaza Foch** and surrounded by hotels, hostels, tour offices, and a dense mix of places to eat and drink. Next door, **La Floresta** has become the city's creative quarter — independent cafés, craft kitchens, street art, and the beloved Ochoymedio arthouse cinema.

It's walkable, central, and well connected, which makes it a practical base for first-timers.

## Getting there from Quito airport (UIO)

La Mariscal is in central Quito, roughly 45 minutes to an hour from **UIO** depending on traffic. A private transfer drops you right at your hotel with your bags — no late-night taxi negotiation after a long flight. With a **flat rate**, the price is set before you land, and your driver tracks your flight.

It's covered by the prepaid $100 [anywhere in Quito](/quito-airport-to-quito) rate (Hyatt, Swissôtel, Oro Verde, and other city hotels) — see that page or the [rates](/#rates).

## Tips

- Great base for **nightlife and dining**; choose the [Old Town](/guide/quito-old-town-guide) instead if you're after history and atmosphere.
- Keep normal **city street-smarts** at night, as in any nightlife district.
- It's central for day trips to [Mitad del Mundo](/guide/visiting-mitad-del-mundo) and the [TelefériQo](/guide/teleferico-quito-pichincha).

Book the airport leg in advance and you arrive ready to enjoy the neighborhood, not wrestle with logistics.
