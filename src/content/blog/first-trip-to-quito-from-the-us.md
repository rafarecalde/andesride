A first trip to Quito from the United States is an ordinary international flight plus two local facts: the airport is well outside the city, and the city is high. This page is what to confirm, what to pack, and how the ride from Mariscal Sucre (UIO) works once you land.

Schedules and entry rules below are a snapshot or a caution. Recheck the airline and the official government pages close to departure.

## Flights US travelers actually search

Nonstops people look up most often are from **Miami, Houston, Atlanta, Fort Lauderdale, and New York (JFK)**. Which of those are operating, and the minute they land, moves by season. Your ticket beats any article.

Two evening arrivals are written up from public schedules checked in early October 2026, with a note to confirm them:

- [American AA833 from Miami](/guide/american-airlines-aa833-miami-to-quito-night), typically around 8:40–9:00pm local in that snapshot.
- [United UA1002 from Houston](/guide/united-ua1002-houston-to-quito-night), typically around 11:25pm local in that snapshot, with a published shift of a few minutes later from late October 2026.

Atlanta, Fort Lauderdale, and JFK show up in the same searches. Around that early-October 2026 check, a Delta nonstop from Atlanta was an early-evening arrival, and an Avianca arrival from JFK was in the early morning. A Fort Lauderdale nonstop was not confirmed as current, so do not assume one is operating. Open the airline's site for the time on your date.

Other late banks, including Bogotá and Panama City, are in the [late-arrival guide](/guide/arriving-quito-airport-late-at-night) from the same schedule check. If yours is a night landing, read that before you decide to sort a car at the curb.

## Entry rules: look them up, do not memorize a blog

US citizens travel to Ecuador with a **passport**. Beyond that, treat the details as official-source material: how long you may stay as a tourist, whether a visa is required for your purpose, whether you need proof of onward travel, and whether any vaccination rule applies because of where else you have been. Those points change, and they depend on your passport and your route.

Use a primary source before you fly. The practical pair is the US Department of State's Ecuador information and the Ecuadorian government page that states entry rules for visitors. A travel blog, including this one, is not the authority. Check again the week you leave. Carry the passport, and keep a copy stored separately.

## What to pack for the city, not for a beach

Quito is on the equator and it is not hot in the coastal sense. Days are often mild, evenings are cooler, and the sun is strong because of the elevation. Pack layers you can add and remove: a light jacket, a shirt you can roll, long pants, and a rain layer. Afternoon weather can change. Check a forecast for your dates rather than an average printed in a guide.

Shoes should handle hills. Old Town is cobbled and steep. Sunscreen, a hat, and sunglasses matter more than people expect. Any prescription medicine stays in its original packaging, in your carry-on, with a bit more than the exact number of days.

Ecuador's plugs and household voltage are generally in the same family as the United States, so many US chargers plug in directly. Confirm with a current electrical reference if a device is particular.

Money is simple and still worth a sentence. Ecuador uses the **US dollar**. Bring some small bills. Do not plan on an airport ATM as your only cash after a late flight. The payment detail is in [paying for a Quito airport ride](/guide/paying-for-quito-airport-rides).

## The first day, including the car

Do not schedule a hard activity for the afternoon you land. Central Quito is about 9,350 feet. What that feels like, and what to skip on day one, is in [altitude on arrival](/guide/altitude-in-quito-what-to-expect). The order inside the terminal — immigration, bags, customs, SIM or eSIM, and the driver — is the [arrival guide](/guide/quito-airport-arrival-guide).

Then the drive. About **45–60 minutes** to a city hotel, **25–35** to Cumbayá, **10–15** to Wyndham Quito Airport.
