Tucked into a green gorge below the Tungurahua volcano, Baños de Agua Santa packs more adrenaline and scenery per square kilometre than almost anywhere in Ecuador.

## What to do in Baños

- **Ruta de las Cascadas** — a string of waterfalls along the river, best explored by bike, including the thundering **Pailón del Diablo**.
- **La Casa del Árbol** — the iconic "swing at the end of the world," soaring out over the valley.
- **Thermal hot springs** that give the town its name ("Holy Water Baths").
- **Canyoning, rafting, and ziplining** for the adventurous — and it's a popular gateway onward to the Amazon at Puyo.

It rewards an overnight (or two), though determined travelers do visit in a long day.
