After a long-haul flight, almost nothing beats sinking into a steaming, mineral-rich thermal pool with the páramo rising around you. Papallacta is that place — and it's one of the smartest trips to book for your **arrival or departure day**.

## Why Papallacta

Set high in the mountains east of Quito on the road toward the Amazon, Papallacta is famous for its **thermal hot springs**, fed by the volcanic activity of the surrounding Cayambe-Coca highlands. The resort and public pools range from warm to very hot, with cold plunge options and views over the páramo. At around 3,300 m the air is cool and bracing — which is exactly what makes the hot water so blissful.

It's a soak-and-relax destination: bring a book, plan a long lunch (the local trout is excellent), and unwind.

## Getting there from Quito airport (UIO)

Papallacta lies east of Quito, and from **UIO airport** — already on the eastern side of the valley — it's a scenic climb into the mountains. Because it sits at altitude on a winding road, a **private flat-rate transfer** is the easy call: a fixed price, a driver comfortable with the high-mountain route, and flight tracking if you're heading there straight off a flight.

## Tips

- It's a great **landing-day** stop: ease into the altitude gently while you soak.
- Bring a **swimsuit, sandals, and a warm layer** for between pools — it's cold out of the water.
- The altitude is real; hydrate and move slowly, especially if you just arrived.
- Combine it with onward travel toward Baeza or the Amazon — just ask when you book.

Pre-book the ride and your trip can begin (or end) with a hot soak instead of a scramble at the curb.
