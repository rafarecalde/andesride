Quito's airport (UIO) sits out in Tababela, a good 45–60 minutes from the city. If you have a pre-dawn departure or a short connection, that distance is the enemy — which is why staying close to the airport is one of the best moves a traveler can make.

## Why stay near UIO

The **Tababela and Puembo** area around the airport has grown a cluster of hotels and guesthouses built for exactly this: early flights, late arrivals, and layovers. The payoff:

- **Minutes from the terminal** instead of an hour each way.
- A calmer, **warmer, lower-elevation** valley — a gentler first or last night in Ecuador.
- No pre-dawn dash across the city to make check-in.

It's not where you'll spend a whole vacation, but for the night before or after a flight, it's hard to beat.
