If you want a calmer, warmer, and closer base than central Quito — especially handy near the airport — the **Valle de Tumbaco** is the local secret. Cumbayá and Tumbaco sit lower than the city, so the climate is milder and the pace is more relaxed.

## What's there

Cumbayá has grown into a polished dining and café scene around its central plaza, popular with Quiteños on weekends. Highlights:

- Excellent **restaurants and coffee** without the Old Town crowds.
- The **Chaquiñán** — a flat former railway line turned walking and cycling trail through the valley.
- An easygoing, residential feel that's great for jet-lag recovery or a long layover.

It's also the gateway toward Papallacta and the eastern mountains.

## Getting there from Quito airport (UIO)

This is the **closest populated valley to the airport** — typically only around 25–35 minutes from **UIO**, since both sit on the eastern side of the metro area. That makes Cumbayá a smart choice if you have an early flight, a short stay, or simply don't want to climb all the way up to central Quito after landing.

Cumbayá is outside the two prepaid products (airport hotel vs. Quito city), so [ask for a quote](/#book) rather than using the $100 city rate. Published prepaid rates are on the [rates page](/#rates).

## Tips

- Great pick for **early departures** — you're close to UIO without being in the airport itself.
- Lower elevation than Quito means a **gentler first night** at altitude.
- Use it as a base for [Papallacta's hot springs](/guide/papallacta-hot-springs-guide) or a quiet first day before exploring the [Old Town](/guide/quito-old-town-guide).

Book the short hop from arrivals and you can be sitting down to dinner in the valley within the hour.
