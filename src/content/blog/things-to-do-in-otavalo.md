Otavalo is home to one of South America's largest and most colorful indigenous markets — and it's one of the most popular trips travelers add the moment they land in Quito. Here's how to do it without losing half a day to logistics.

## Why Otavalo is worth the drive

The **Plaza de los Ponchos** market fills the town center with handwoven textiles, alpaca scarves, ponchos, jewelry, and Andean crafts. It runs daily but is biggest and most vibrant on **Saturdays**, when the animal and produce markets come alive too. Otavalo sits at around 2,550 m in a valley ringed by volcanoes, so the air is crisp and the light is beautiful.

Nearby, it's easy to add the **Peguche waterfall**, the **Cuicocha** crater lake, or the leather town of **Cotacachi** to make a full day of it.

## Getting there from Quito airport (UIO)

Otavalo is roughly two hours north of Quito on the Panamericana, climbing through classic Andean scenery. ## Tips for a smooth visit

- Go on a **Saturday** for the full market, but arrive early to beat the crowds and tour buses.
- Bring **cash** in small bills for the market — and haggle politely; it's expected.
- It's high altitude — if you've just arrived in Ecuador, take it easy on day one.
