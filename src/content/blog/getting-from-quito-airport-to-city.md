Quito's airport (UIO) sits about 45–60 minutes from the city in Tababela, so "how do I actually get to my hotel?" is the first real decision of any trip. Here's every option, honestly compared.

## The four ways into the city

**1. Airport shuttle bus (Aeroservicios).** The official airport bus runs to a couple of designated city stops. It's the cheapest option, but it drops you at a fixed point — not your hotel — so you'll still need a taxi or rideshare for the last leg, with your luggage, after a flight. ([More on the bus vs. a transfer.](/guide/aeroservicios-bus-vs-private-transfer))

**2. Airport taxi.** Plentiful and convenient, but the fare to the city isn't fixed and meters aren't always used at the airport — so the price depends on negotiation, time of day, and traffic. ([How much a Quito airport taxi costs.](/guide/how-much-is-a-taxi-from-quito-airport))

**3. Rideshare (Uber/inDrive).** Apps work in Quito, but **airport pickup is restricted and the rules change**, surge pricing kicks in at busy times, and there's no one waiting for you. ([Does Uber work at UIO?](/guide/uber-from-quito-airport))

**4. Private transfer.** A vetted driver meets you at arrivals with your name on a sign, at one **flat USD price set before you fly**, with flight tracking included. It's not the cheapest line item — it's the one with no surprises.

## How to choose

- **On a tight budget, traveling light, arriving in daylight?** The bus can work.
- **Want certainty, arriving at night, carrying luggage, or traveling as a family?** A prepaid luxury-SUV transfer is the easy call — **$100** anywhere in Quito city, **$50** to Wyndham Quito Airport. See the [rates](/#rates).
- **Comfortable with apps and some uncertainty?** Rideshare can be cheaper off-peak, if pickup is allowed when you land.

## The honest summary

The trade-off is **price vs. certainty**. The cheaper options cost less money but more time, hassle, and risk — exactly the things you least want after a long flight at altitude. A pre-booked flat-rate transfer costs more than a bus ticket and buys away all of it.
