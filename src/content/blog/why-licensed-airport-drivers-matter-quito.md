The cheapest car outside the terminal isn't always the safest — and in an unfamiliar country, that trade-off matters more than a few dollars.

## Licensed vs. informal rides

Ecuador regulates commercial passenger transport, and the airport has its own rules for who may pick up arriving passengers. Licensed, airport-authorized drivers carry the proper authority, commercial plates, and insurance. Informal ("pirata") cars often carry none of that — which becomes your problem if anything goes wrong.

## What you're actually paying for

A pre-booked, licensed transfer means a vetted driver, a vehicle that's insured to carry you, a fixed price set in advance, and accountability if something isn't right. That's the certainty premium — and it's the whole point of booking ahead instead of gambling at the curb.
