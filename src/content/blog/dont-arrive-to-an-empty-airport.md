There's a specific kind of stress in walking out of customs into a crowd of unfamiliar faces, in a country where you don't speak the language, looking for a ride that may or may not exist. It is sharper after a **late US inbound** — many Miami flights into UIO are around **11:30pm** — when the hall is thinner and you are too tired to negotiate.

## Meet & greet removes the guesswork

With a pre-booked transfer, your driver is standing at the arrivals exit holding a sign with your name. You walk straight to them. No apps, no fare haggling, no wondering which car is yours — and help with your bags to the vehicle. The chauffeur holds the SUV door. That is the product: safety, reliability, comfort — not the cheapest curb fare.

## Why it beats hailing on the spot

A driver who's expecting you has your flight number, knows when you actually land, and has already been paid the agreed flat rate in USD. Compare that to scanning a taxi line at midnight and hoping the car is legitimate. We do not compete with street taxis on price; we charge a prepaid premium so you are not bargaining at 11:30pm. ## What to expect

- Look for your name on a sign just past the arrivals doors.
- Your driver helps with luggage and opens the door.
- The price is already set — $50 airport hotel, $75 anywhere in Cumbayá, $100 anywhere in Quito. Nothing to negotiate.
